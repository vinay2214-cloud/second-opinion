# Second Opinion: tester script

Thank you for testing. This takes about 20 minutes. You need a **Meta Quest 3 or 3S**, a table, and a chair. No controllers: the game uses your hands.

**Link:** https://vinay2214-cloud.github.io/second-opinion/

Please do not read about the game first. The point is to see what a new player does.

## Before you start
1. Sit at a table with about 60 cm of clear space in front of you. Move drinks away.
2. Start a recording of your view **while you still hold the right controller**: press the Meta button on the right controller to open the menu, select **Camera**, then select **Record video**. A red dot appears in the headset when recording starts.
3. Put both controllers down. The game uses your hands only. If your hands do not appear after a few seconds, pick up the right controller, open the menu, and go to **Quick controls**, **Settings**, **Hands and body**, and switch **Hand and body tracking** on.
4. Please say what you are thinking as you play. Whether your voice is captured in the recording depends on a Camera setting. *Unverified, ask your own headset:* look in the Camera settings for a microphone option. If you cannot find one, written notes afterwards are fine.

Opening the menu with hands only: look at your palm at eye level, hold your thumb and index finger together until the button icon fills, then release.

## Run 1: normal
1. Open the link in the **Quest Browser** (the built-in browser).
2. Tap **Enter XR** and allow the permissions it asks for.
3. A glowing pad appears on your table. Pinch it to place the game. If no pad appears within a few seconds, the game places a virtual table for you.
4. Play **Module 1** to the end (about 3 minutes). It has a short tutorial.
5. Then play **Module 3** once (about 7 minutes).
6. **Think aloud** the whole time: say what you expect, what you are trying to do, and what confuses you. Do not look anything up. If you are stuck for a minute, say so and move on.

## Run 2: with the debug panel (2 minutes)
1. Open this link instead: https://vinay2214-cloud.github.io/second-opinion/?debug=1
2. Enter XR and place the game again. A small purple panel appears to the **left** of the big text panel. It shows the build tag, frames per second, hand tracking state and the last error.
3. Start Module 3 and wait until two patients are on the table at once (about two and a half minutes in).
4. Take a photo with the purple panel in view: open the menu (Meta button on the right controller, or the palm pinch described above), select **Camera**, then select **Take photo**. A blinking red dot shows that the photo is being taken. With the right controller in hand there is also a shortcut: hold the Meta button, then press and release the trigger.
5. **Write down the build tag** shown on the first line of the purple panel (for example `rc1`) and send it with your feedback, so we know which build you played.

## If it stutters or things look wrong
Open https://vinay2214-cloud.github.io/second-opinion/?debug=1&safe=1 and try again. This turns off see-through effects. Tell us whether it helped.

## Then
1. Stop the recording: open the menu, select **Camera**, then select **Recording**.
2. Find your files on the headset in **Files** (under **Media** or **Recents**) or in the **Gallery** app. *Unverified, ask your own headset:* how you get them off the headset (the Meta Horizon phone app, a USB cable to a computer, or a share option in Gallery) differs by setup; use whichever you normally use.
3. Fill in `feedback-form.md` (copy it into a message or a document).
4. Send back: the recording, the debug photo, the build tag, and the form.

Menu names above follow Meta's help pages as read on 11 Nov 2026 (links below). Headset software updates often rename things; if a name differs on your headset, use the closest match.
- Record video: https://www.meta.com/help/quest/516228089650875/
- Take a photo: https://www.meta.com/help/quest/615186776533677/
- Hand tracking and the palm pinch: https://www.meta.com/help/quest/290147772643252/
