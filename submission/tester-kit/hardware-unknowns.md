# What only a real headset can tell us

These could not be checked in the emulator. Please pay attention to them; each maps to a question in the feedback form.

| Unknown | What to look for | Form question |
|---|---|---|
| Table detection in a real room | Does the pad appear on your table, at table height, within about 5 s? | 1, 2 |
| Anchor drift | Does the game stay fixed to the table for the whole shift, also after looking away? | 3 |
| Pinch jitter on the small parked token | Can you re-grab a token that is already on a tray? | 5 |
| Poke accuracy on the smallest buttons | Keep, Menu, Pause and Centre are 4 to 7 cm wide with 2 cm gaps. Any mis-pokes? | 6 |
| Readability at about 65 cm | The big panel is the farthest text. Comfortable to read? | 10 |
| Readability of small labels | Button labels, advisor names, the "update installed" tag | 11, 12 |
| False tracking-loss freeze | Does the game freeze when a hand rests on the table or leaves view briefly? | 8 |
| Frame rate | The number on the debug panel, especially with two patients on the table | 14 |
| Neck angle | The card is on the table and the big panel is upright. Too much looking up and down? | 13 |
| Load time | From tapping the link to seeing Enter XR | 15 |
